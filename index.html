AO Trust Bridge Prototypes

Two clickable prototypes exploring a persistent "trust bridge" that carries customers from a completed phone purchase through the data plan and insurance checkout journeys. Both are faithful to the AO design language.

Version A

Progress & Completion

The trust bridge reinforces momentum. It leads with reassurance, a visible progress bar, and a clear step tracker so the customer always feels how close they are to the finish line.

  • Progress bar & step indicators front and centre
  • "Just 2 quick steps left" momentum copy
  • Completed steps become visually confirmed
  • Celebratory success state at 100%

Version B

Benefits & Value

The trust bridge leads with value — savings, protection, convenience, and peace of mind. Progress tracking is still present, but kept secondary beneath the benefit messaging.

  • Benefit & savings messaging up front
  • Bundle & peace-of-mind copy per screen
  • Subtle secondary progress dots
